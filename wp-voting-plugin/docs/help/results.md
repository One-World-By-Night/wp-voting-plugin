# Vote Results

What a vote's result page shows, while it's open and once it's counted.

## Who can use this

- Anyone who can see the vote, once it has closed.
- While it's open, if the vote shows live results.
- Site admins and whoever created the vote, always.

## How to get there

council.owbn.net → **Vote Results** → the vote, or **Results** under it in wp-admin → **WP Voting** → **All Votes**. A vote that's still open shows its live count on its own page, under the ballot.

## The screen

- The vote's title, dates, and who proposed and seconded it.
- **Total votes** and when the result was **Calculated**.
- **Export CSV** - the vote and every ballot, for site admins.
- The outcome: the winner or winners, **Passed** or **Did Not Pass**, or a tie.
- A table of each option's votes and share.
- By type:
  - Ranked votes: **Round-by-Round Results**, with who was eliminated each round.
  - Multi-seat votes: each seat and who filled it.
  - **Condorcet Method**: the **Head-to-Head Matrix** and **Final Rankings**.
  - **Disciplinary Vote**: the **Cascade (most → least severe)**, with each level's votes and whether it met its threshold.
  - **Consent Agenda**: **Passed by Consent**, or the objections filed and the **Objectors**.
- **Voter Comments**, when the vote took them.
- **Voter List** and **Participation**: who voted, under which role, and who hasn't.
- The **Admin Note**, when it's shown publicly.

While a vote is open with live results on, the page says "These are live results" and keeps counting.

## Common tasks

### See how a vote went

1. On **Vote Results**, find the vote and open it.
2. On a ranked vote, read the rounds to see where each ballot went.

### Download the ballots

1. On the result page, click **Export CSV**.

## Things to know

- On an anonymous vote, the counts show but not who chose what.
- **Showing votes cast under your current eligible roles** means you see the ballots your roles let you see, not all of them.
- A result is counted once, when the vote closes. A site admin can count late ones from **Settings** → **Advanced** → **Process All Closed Votes**.

## Troubleshooting

- **No result on a closed vote.** It hasn't been counted yet; it will be within the hour.
- **No Export CSV button.** It's for site admins.
- **A ranked result looks wrong.** Follow the rounds: who dropped out, and where their ballots moved.
