# Voting

How CMs, coordinators and the others a vote names cast their ballot on council.owbn.net.

## Who can use this

Members whose roles a vote names under who can vote, such as every chronicle's CM. Signed in.

## How to get there

council.owbn.net → **Voting Dashboard**.

## The screen

- The open and scheduled votes you can see, with **Title**, **Proposed By**, **Type** (the proposal type, such as Budget), **Start**, **End**, **Votes**, **Status** and **Result**.
- Search and filter boxes above the list, and **Show … per page**.
- In **Result**: **Vote** on an open vote you can vote on; **Update** on one you've already voted on, if changing is allowed; **Object** on a consent agenda item; **View** for the rest.
- A scheduled vote shows when it opens.

The ballot opens over the list:

- The options, as the vote's type asks: pick one, drag them into order (**Move up** and **Move down** do the same), pick a punishment level, or file an objection.
- **You have multiple eligible roles. Select which role you are voting as** - when more than one of your roles can vote, such as a CM who is also a coordinator. Your ballot counts for that chronicle or office.
- **Comment (optional)** - when the vote takes comments.
- **Email me a confirmation of my vote** - when the vote allows it, with the addresses to send it to.
- **Submit Vote**, **Update Vote** or **File Objection**.

## Common tasks

### Vote

1. On the **Voting Dashboard**, click **Vote** next to the vote.
2. Make your choice. On a ranked vote, drag your first choice to the top; you don't have to rank them all.
3. If asked, pick the role you're voting as.
4. Click **Submit Vote**.

### Change your vote

1. Click **Update** next to the vote. Your earlier choice is filled in.
2. Change it and click **Update Vote**.

This only works while the vote is open, and only if the vote allows changes.

### Object to a consent agenda item

1. Click **Object** next to the item.
2. Add a comment if you like, and click **File Objection**.

Your objection turns the item into a full Approve / Deny / Abstain vote straight away, open for a week. If you have no objection, do nothing: the item passes when its review ends.

## Things to know

- You cast one ballot per vote. Voting again, where changes are allowed, replaces it.
- Each chronicle or office has one ballot. If someone else already voted for your chronicle or office, your ballot replaces theirs: the latest one counts.
- Votes close on their closing date, and the count runs within the hour.
- On a vote with live results, you can watch the count while it's open.

## Troubleshooting

- **No Vote button.** The vote isn't open yet, you've already voted and changes aren't allowed, or none of your roles can vote on it.
- **"You do not have permission to vote on this proposal."** None of your roles is on the vote's list. If you were given the role recently, sign out and in again.
- **"You must be logged in to vote."** Sign in to council.owbn.net.
- **A vote you expect isn't listed.** It's restricted to roles you don't hold, or it's still a draft. Finished votes are on the **Vote Results** page.
